What SheGuard will and won’t tell you

  • It will show distance, time and alternatives from Google’s routing data.
  • It will show recorded safety data only when an official source supplies it, with the source, date and coverage next to it.
  • It will say “Insufficient safety data” instead of inventing a score.
  • It never calls a route guaranteed safe. No record of incidents does not mean no risk.
  • A report saved here is not a police complaint unless an agency integration says otherwise.

What you can do

Safe routes

Compare alternative routes, see nearby services, and open your pick in Google Maps.

Report an incident

Save a private report with optional evidence and track its status.

Emergency services

Official helpline numbers, nearby stations and hospitals, and an SOS to contacts who agreed to receive it.

Your data, your control

Location is shared only when you tick the box. You can delete your account data in Settings.

In an emergency call 112. SheGuard is a planning and reporting aid, not an emergency service.
